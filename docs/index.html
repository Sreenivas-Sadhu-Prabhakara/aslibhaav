Why it's hard to eyeball

A 10% trade discount and a 5% cash discount don't add to 15% — they compound. "Buy 10 get 2 free" quietly spreads your cost over 12 units. Freight can be on top or already inside the price. Aslibhaav does the compounding so two schemes with different shapes become comparable.

  1. Trade then cash discount, applied in sequence.
  2. Free goods dilute cost across X + Y received units.
  3. Extra freight is allocated per received unit.

Built as Flutter → Go → PostgreSQL. The Go middleware owns the same calculation and keeps a history of what each scheme really cost.
